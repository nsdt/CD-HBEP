CD-HBEP (core formulation)
Hermite beam: the input contains only (x,y), and the slopes θ are computed internally.
CD-HBEP projects the unconstrained Hermite beam slope θB onto a finite feasible set. Exact interval certification activates the bounds. Bezier control values define the envelope constraints. An excess-total-variation test activates the sharp Hermite slope box, with a wider automatic threshold for isolated shallow reversals.
min 1/2(θ-θB)THB(θ-θB), θ∈FA. The feasible slope box FA contains the activated bounds.
CD-HBEP applies the same twelve fixed activation constants to every data set and requires no case-specific or user tuning. The controls below expose those constants for inspection and reproducibility; the default values are the manuscript settings.

The manuscript treats the guards and the twelve empirical activation constants below as fixed parts of CD-HBEP. The values remain editable here for inspection and reproducibility. The guard switches support debugging and ablation checks.
The chord-side guard uses the available adjacent secant-slope differences only when their nonzero signs agree. A common factor enlarges all secondary slacks when necessary to preserve feasibility with the primary envelope and monotonicity bounds. Its analytical upper bound is max{1, 1/(3ω_side^loc)}; the default ω_side^loc = 0.20 gives 5/3.
No full curvature-sign constraint, no collocation envelope constraint, no hard chord cap, and no local slope prior are used by default.

Notes

Test cases. The manuscript presets are Surge (classical Akima data), Peak (titanium equation-of-state data), Hump (Manni numerical example), Arc (Kvasov semicircle data), and four synthetic stress cases: Oscillatory, Turn, Wiggle, and Plateau.
Hyman-filtered spline uses not-a-knot cubic-spline derivative estimates followed by the Hyman 1983 Eq. (2.3) monotonicity filter. At changing-monotonicity knots the filtered derivative is set to zero.
HG-MDO denotes the Han–Guo minimal derivative oscillation method. It determines all cubic Hermite slopes by minimizing the integrated squared difference between the derivative of the interpolant and the derivative of the piecewise-linear interpolant. It solves Eq. (3) of Han and Guo (2018) on uniform or nonuniform knots. The method has no shape constraint, so overshoot or reverse motion can remain after optimization.
DEH cubic monotone (NC init.) uses natural-cubic-spline derivative estimates as the initial derivatives and applies the Dougherty–Edelman–Hyman cubic monotonicity constrainer. This is a representative value-only implementation of the cubic monotone part of the DEH paper.
Thesis-style rod FEM interpolation is a historical reconstruction model based on the graduation thesis. The input values are fixed displacements, and unknown internal displacements are computed from a rod stiffness system with force interpolation. This optional implementation illustrates the design lineage of CD-HBEP and is not one of the manuscript's principal comparison methods.
CD-HBEP fixes the data values and computes Hermite slopes internally. It projects the Hermite beam slope θB onto the finite bounds activated after exact interval certification detects a visible envelope escape, reverse motion, or one-sided chord excursion.
